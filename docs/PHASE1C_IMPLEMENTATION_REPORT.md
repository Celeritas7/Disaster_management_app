## Record and compatibility decisions

- **No breaking migration:** all new fields are optional within version 1. `night.leaveAt` stores a manually chosen target timestamp (in epoch milliseconds) for the current session. Older live sessions keep their existing numbers and do not pretend to have a target date.
- Activity entries may carry `kind` (`manual`, `departure`, `stranded`, `incident`, `night`, `reflection`) and `status` (`recorded`, `confirmed`, `resolved`, `reviewed`, `saved`). Old records receive `legacy/unknown` **in memory** and become explicit once the user next saves, without losing their note text.
- The optional timer uses the Japan Standard Time offset (+09:00). It never rolls a missed leave target to another day after it is set. An alcohol-free night is allowed with a limit of zero.
- No contact names, address strings or timeline entries are generated by sample data; all displayed personal data comes from the user’s records.
- **Shared-origin safety:** the service worker now owns `disaster-management-app-v6`; it still deletes only `disaster-management-app-vN` caches. Sibling apps such as KG Chart are unaffected by intended cache operations.

## Source and safety review

The original mock included fabricated train timing, historical nights, calendar events, “Fires” counters, and personally specific alcohol-poisoning anecdotes. These were deliberately **not** copied as real states.

The urgent intoxication screen restores original bilingual warning labels and a `tel:119` **dialer link**. The principle of seeking emergency help for unconsciousness, abnormal breathing, vomiting, and concerning skin color was checked against [NHS guidance on alcohol poisoning](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/alcohol-poisoning/) and [Japan Fire and Disaster Management Agency guidance on calling 119](https://www.fdma.go.jp/laws/tutatsu/items/804b3720c39af58d6dfea787400048b215e3d69e.pdf). This is not a medical certification of the UI, and clinical review remains a Phase 5 / Phase 10 release gate. A browser tap does not place a call or confirm dispatch.
